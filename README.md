# Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance across products, cities, orders, quantities, and payment methods.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an executive-level sales dashboard built with Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard provides a clear view of overall sales performance and allows users to explore revenue distribution across different products, cities, and payment methods.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was developed to:

- Monitor overall revenue and sales performance.
- Track total quantity and number of orders.
- Compare revenue across different products.
- Analyze city-level sales performance.
- Understand revenue distribution by payment method.
- Provide an interactive and easy-to-use executive reporting interface.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Revenue Analysis
- Total Revenue KPI

- Revenue by City
- Revenue by Payment Method

### Sales Analysis
- Total Quantity
- Total Orders
- Product-level performance
- City-level performance

### Interactive Filters
Users can dynamically filter the dashboard by:

- City
- Payment Method
- Product

### Detailed Analysis

The dashboard also includes a matrix that breaks down revenue by:

- City
- Beverages
- Burgers
- Chicken Sandwiches
- Fries
- Sides & Other
- Total Revenue
